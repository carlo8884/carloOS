**Subject:** Ran an AI review of the site — a few ideas, take 'em or leave 'em

Hey Glen,

I ran an AI review of StateLineTack to see where there might be some easy wins, and it came back with a handful of ideas. I wanted to pass them along — totally up to you whether any are useful. Use them, ignore them, or tell me what you think. I'm really just the messenger here; none of this is me telling you how to run things, it's all the AI's read on the site. A couple even came out as finished, ready-to-use pieces.

Here's what it found:

**1. A blanket size calculator** *(the AI actually built this one — attached)*
A little tool where a customer enters their horse's measurement and it tells them the exact blanket size to buy. Open the attachment in any browser to see it. The thinking: wrong-size blanket returns are expensive — we eat shipping both ways — so if people get the size right the first time, returns drop. Your team could drop it onto a page in a few minutes, and it can be styled to match the site.

**2. An official "deals" page**
Right now when someone Googles "StateLineTack coupon," they land on third-party coupon sites (RetailMeNot, Honey, etc.) — usually full of dead codes, and tools like Honey can skim commission off a sale we already made. The idea is to have our own official deals page that ranks for those searches with real current offers, so that traffic (and that money) stays with us instead of the middlemen.

**3. Star ratings and price showing in Google**
Our products show up in Google as plain blue links, while a lot of competitors show star ratings and price right in the results. There's a small, standard bit of code that makes our reviews and price display the same way — same rankings we already have, just more clicks because the listing stands out. (It only shows stars if a product actually has real reviews — nothing made up.)

**4. Recovering lost traffic from the old site**
When the site moved to Shopify, a bunch of old page links got left behind. Google still sends people to some of them and they hit dead pages — so we're losing visitors and the ranking those pages had. The AI built a tool that finds all those broken links and produces a ready-to-upload list to send them to the right new pages. The only thing it'd need from you is a quick 2-minute export from Google Search Console, then I run it and hand you the file.

That's it — none of these need a big project or any spend, and you can take them one at a time or not at all. If any sound useful I'm happy to explain more or just hand it over ready to go. And if you think the AI's off base on any of it, tell me — genuinely no pressure either way.

— [you]
