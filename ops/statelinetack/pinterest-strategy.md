# StateLineTack / equestrian Pinterest strategy

> Channel rationale: equestrian retail is one of the most Pinterest-native categories (planning-heavy, visual, seasonal, high purchase intent). It's an **owned channel** — no Glen sign-off, no ad budget required to start. Pins are evergreen and compound (a pin can drive traffic for years), which fits the "every asset compounds" charter.
>
> **Funnel rule:** pins point to our **Saddle.com / Horses.com** guide+tool pages first (where we capture email + own the relationship), which then refer to StateLineTack — *not* cold-linking pins straight to a product. This builds our asset while sending SLT qualified buyers.
>
> Division of labor: **this doc = strategy + all copy** (titles, descriptions, keywords, calendar, page mapping). **Graphics + posting = design tool / Codex / human** (I can't generate pin images or post). The graphic spec at the bottom hands that off cleanly.

## 1. Board architecture

Two axes — **by category** and **by use-case/season**. Use-case boards out-perform category boards for saves, so lead with them.

**Use-case / seasonal boards (priority):**
- Winter Horse Blanketing
- Fly Season Protection (sheets, masks, boots)
- New Horse Owner Essentials
- Horse Sizing & Fit Guides
- Barn Organization & Tack Room
- Horse Show Prep & Turnout
- Senior Horse Care
- Trail Riding Gear

**Category boards:**
- Horse Blankets & Sheets
- Saddle Pads & Numnahs
- Bits, Bridles & Tack
- Horse Boots & Leg Protection
- Equestrian Apparel
- Grooming & Health

Each board: keyword-rich title + 1–2 sentence description with primary keyword. Example board description (Winter Horse Blanketing): *"How to choose, fit, and size horse blankets for winter. Turnout vs. stable blankets, fill weights, temperature guides, and measuring how-tos."*

## 5. Cadence & mechanics
- **5–10 pins/week** to start; consistency > volume. Re-pin evergreens across relevant boards (no duplicate URLs same-day).
- **Fresh pins win:** new image + new title to the *same* URL counts as fresh — get multiple pins per landing page over weeks.
- **Rich Pins:** enable (validates Open Graph on the landing pages — free distribution boost). Requires the OG tags our Saddle/Horses pages already ship.
- **Standard vertical 2:3** (1000×1500). Idea pins for fit how-tos (native reach).

## 6. Graphic spec — handoff to design tool / Codex
*(I write the brief; the design tool/Codex/human produces the images.)*

- **Format:** 1000×1500 px (2:3), <20 MB, PNG/JPG.
- **Template families:**
  1. *How-to/diagram* (measuring, fit) — clean photo or line diagram + bold title bar.
  2. *Chart/guide* (weight chart, size conversion) — table teaser + "free guide" badge.
  3. *Checklist* (winter care, new owner) — list teaser, "PIN THIS" cue.
- **Type:** title legible at thumbnail; high contrast; brand accent `#1f6f54` (match tool).
- **No fake humans/vets, no stripped photographer credit** (QC §1 — applies to any photography sourced).
- **Text on image:** ≤ 6–8 words; full keyword goes in the pin title/description, not buried in art.
- Deliver as a numbered set matching the pin table in §2 so titles/descriptions attach 1:1.
