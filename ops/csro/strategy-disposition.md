## §1 The two value paths (every in-scope domain is assigned one)

Carlo wants each domain to resolve to **SELL** or **EARN**. I split that into 6 concrete dispositions:

| Disposition | Meaning | Monetization |
|---|---|---|
| **SELL-NOW (groom)** | Already has acquirer interest; maximize price, then exit | Sale proceeds |
| **BUILD-TO-EARN** | Build into a real revenue asset; hold and compound | Affiliate / lead-gen / tools |
| **BUILD-TO-FLIP** | Build just enough traffic+revenue to sell at a multiple | Sale at 30–45× monthly profit |
| **REDIRECT** | Fold into a hero domain (kill duplication, pass authority) | Indirect (boosts hero) |
| **SELL-DOMAIN** | Thin/off-thesis; worth more as a domain name than a site | Domain marketplace sale/lease |
| **SET-ASIDE** | Personal / other-business / out of scope | n/a |

**Exit-multiple reality `[RESEARCH — to verify w/ comps, R-003]`:** content/affiliate sites trade ~30–45× trailing
monthly net profit (Empire Flippers / FE International range). So **revenue and the for-sale decision are the same
project** — a site earning $2k/mo sells for ~$70–90k; the same domain parked sells for domain-value only. Building
revenue *is* how we raise sale price. This unifies Carlo's "sell or earn" into one motion: **earn first, then the
sale price follows.**

## §5 Go-live readiness gate `[CSRO DECISION]` — nothing goes to DNS until it clears

Carlo's rule: no site goes live until it can generate traffic. A domain passes the gate only when **all** are true:

1. **Trust-bar clean** — QC-STANDARDS §1 (no fabricated authority, no AI humans, FTC disclosure surfaced). Non-negotiable.
2. **Substantive content** — a real hub + cluster (not thin programmatic); enough depth to rank and to be citable.
3. **Technical SEO** — unique titles/descriptions, JSON-LD schema, internal link graph, sitemap + robots.
4. **Monetization wired OR sell-track clear** — at least one revenue surface live (affiliate/lead-gen) with
   disclosure, *or* the site is explicitly being groomed for sale with the metrics that supports.
5. **Visual baseline** — Visual Bot sign-off (not placeholder).

Sites that can't clear (2) soon should be re-dispositioned to SELL-DOMAIN rather than launched thin.
