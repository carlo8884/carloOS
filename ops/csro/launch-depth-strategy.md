# Launch-Depth Strategy — evidence-based (2026-06-01)

**Author:** CSRO · **Trigger:** Carlo challenged "launch ferret.com with 23 pages." He was right.
**Method:** 5-angle deep research (topical authority, content clusters, first-impression/credibility,
GEO/AI-citation, affiliate-earning thresholds + the counter-case). Sources cited inline.

---

## TL;DR (the decision)
**Do NOT launch any site thin (~20 pages). Build to cluster-depth first.**
**Floor: ~50–80 substantive, fact-dense, interlinked pages across COMPLETE clusters.**
Bigger is better **only if every page is genuinely substantive** — padded/thin-AI pages at scale
actively *hurt* (site-level quality classifier). Visual identity must land in parallel (50ms test).

This reverses the earlier CSRO lean toward "launch thin, iterate live." Carlo's instinct was correct.

---

## The evidence (confidence-tagged)

### Depth speeds ranking — and there's no penalty for launching big
- **Graphite controlled study (332 URLs):** high topical authority = **57% faster to first
  visibility, 62% more likely to get traffic in week 1.** [HIGH — disclosed methodology]
  https://graphite.io/five-percent/topical-authority-white-paper
- **Google/John Mueller (on record):** launching thousands of pages at once is "generally not an
  issue"; artificially trickling content "causes more problems than it solves." → building deep
  before launch has **zero SEO downside.** [HIGHEST — Google primary]
  https://www.seroundtable.com/google-mass-publishing-seo-24110.html

### A thin site loses the visitor permanently (the human argument — Carlo's point)
- **First impression forms in 50ms and is stable/hard to reverse** (Lindgaard 2006, peer-reviewed;
  primacy + anchoring + halo effects). [HIGH — peer-reviewed]
- **Return rate: 1 page = 8%, 2 pages = 22% — ~3× lift from depth** (Chartbeat). A site that feels
  empty doesn't get a second visit. [HIGH — large behavioral dataset]
- **Complication:** the 50ms *credibility* judgment keys off **visual design first** (Stanford/Fogg,
  n=2,684; 46–75%). → need depth AND visual polish. A bland deep site still fails the first test.

### The ONE real risk is thin-at-scale, NOT page count
- **Helpful Content classifier is site-level** — thin pages drag down the good ones. March 2024
  update suppressed **45% of unoriginal/templated content.** [HIGHEST — Google primary]
  → 200 padded pages = worse than 50 great ones. Quality is the gate, not volume.
- Every penalized case study shares one fingerprint: **unedited, unoriginal, templated at scale.**

### Earning + AI-citation thresholds
- **Amazon:** 10-post approval floor + **3 sales / 180 days or account closes** (live clock since
  2026-05-31). [HIGH — Amazon policy]
- **Income bands (real but n=1 reports):** ~30–50 pages = legitimate + early rankings; **~100 =
  first few-$K/mo**; ~300 = consistent $5K+/mo. [MEDIUM]
- **AI citations reward fact-density + clusters, NOT length** (Princeton GEO paper, ACM KDD 2024,
  ~10k queries): +statistics 41%, +sources up to 115%, *"more words alone = no improvement."*
  [HIGH — peer-reviewed] https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735
- **Timeline floor is structural:** ~60–90 day "sandbox" lag + 6–12 mo earning ramp **regardless of
  page count** — so the count you launch with doesn't change *when* the clock starts, only how much
  surface area is compounding once it does. Reason to build now, not to delay launch indefinitely.

## The rule (applies to all 15 sites, not just ferret)

| Gate | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Page-count floor** | ~50–80 substantive pages, structured as complete clusters (hub→spoke) |
| **Per-cluster depth** | ~10–15 interlinked spokes per hub (practitioner consensus) |
| **Quality bar** | Every page fact-dense (≥1 verifiable fact / ~80 words), cited, QC §1 clean. NO thin filler — it's net-negative |
| **Structure** | hub → cluster → spoke, reciprocal internal links, JSON-LD on every page |
| **Visual** | identity + hero + real photos landed in parallel (50ms credibility test) |
| **Then** | launch. Don't keep delaying past the floor — sandbox clock is the same regardless |

**The trap to avoid (both directions):** launching thin (loses the visitor + weak authority) AND
mass-publishing thin-to-hit-a-number (triggers site-level suppression). The only winning move is
*depth made of genuinely good pages.*
