visitor structure for the site. → Monetization Bot directive when PetFood is in the build queue.

## §2 What content actually earns (high-intent × rising demand) `[RESEARCH]`

1. **Fresh-food comparison/"vs" pages** — Farmer's Dog vs Ollie vs Spot & Tango vs Nom Nom. Fresh dog-food sales
   **+86% since 2021**; this SERP is owned by Dogster/K9ofMine/DeliveryRank — beatable with better, sourced content.
   **Matches the $50–100 payouts.** ← build first.
2. **Gut-health / supplement explainers** — gut health = **#1 new-product claim (31% of launches)**; 69% of buyers
   want functional toppers. High commercial intent (supplement affiliates) + GEO-citable.
3. **DCM / grain-free explainer** — FDA *paused* public updates, no causal link; topic stays contested. **Strong GEO
   play:** AI surfaces want a calibrated, FDA+AVMA-sourced, non-alarmist answer few sites provide. Citation magnet.
4. **Novel-protein cluster** (duck/rabbit/venison/insect/BSFL) — low-competition, rising, schema-friendly
   programmatic surface (protein × condition matrix) per CarloOS framework.
5. **Food-sensitivity / allergen guides** — authority-hub + high-intent (drives to limited-ingredient brands).

> Trust-bar: DCM/health content must be primary-source-cited (FDA, AVMA), calibrated, no fabricated authority (QC §1).

## §3 Buyer map — who has budget + who acquires `[RESEARCH]`

**Advertiser/affiliate targets = the brands spending at Petfood Forum 2026:** Blue Buffalo (General Mills),
Champion Petfoods, Hill's (Colgate), Mars Petcare, Nestlé Purina, Well Pet + the DTC-fresh set above.

**Acquirer landscape (the eventual buyer set for a built PetFood.com):**
- **General Mills (Blue Buffalo)** — most active roll-up: bought Whitebridge ($1.4B) + Tyson pet-treats ($1.2B),
  pushing into *fresh*. The hungriest strategic.
- Nestlé Purina · Mars Petcare · Colgate (Hill's) · Post Holdings (bought Smucker's pet brands) · Chewy (PE-controlled).
- **⚠️ KEY FINDING — no pet-MEDIA roll-up exists.** Observable M&A is all **brand/manufacturer**, not content/media.
  **Implication:** PetFood.com exits to a **strategic CPG/brand buyer wanting owned audience + SEO/GEO real estate**
  (or a generic content/affiliate roll-up like EF) — NOT a dedicated pet-media consolidator (there isn't one).
  This is the same lesson as Horses.com: build the *audience + revenue*, the strategic buyer wants the asset, not the niche.
