## Sequencing (so it compounds, not dumps)
1. **Wave 1 — finish HEALTH + CARE** (already strongest; ~16 new spokes) → these are your authority core.
2. **Wave 2 — DIET cluster** (~12, commercial intent) → wire buy-boxes here = revenue surface.
3. **Wave 3 — BEHAVIOR + BREEDS/COLORS** (~19, top-of-funnel + "fun stuff").
4. **Wave 4 — OWNERSHIP/LIFESTYLE** (~9, decision-stage + email capture + Ferrets.com cross-link).
5. **In parallel (Visual):** identity/logo/hero + real photos on every hub + Tier-1 polish (50ms test).
6. **In parallel (Monetization):** fix the 59 untracked links + DIET-cluster buy-boxes + /go routing.
7. **Pre-launch gate:** ≥70 pages, every cluster reciprocal-linked, JSON-LD everywhere, QC §1 clean,
   visual identity landed, affiliate-link-integrity = 0 untracked. THEN Carlo does DNS + Amazon tag.

## Quality bar (non-negotiable per launch-depth-strategy.md)
Every page: fact-dense (≥1 verifiable fact / ~80 words), cited, original, hub+spoke interlinked,
schema'd. NO thin filler — a padded page is net-negative (site-level classifier). Better 70 great
than 120 mixed.

## Effort estimate
~55–65 net-new pages. At parallel multi-agent dispatch (non-overlapping clusters per agent, per
CLAUDE.md §7), this is days, not weeks — but each must clear QC. This is the real pre-launch work,
and it's why the launch was right to hold.
