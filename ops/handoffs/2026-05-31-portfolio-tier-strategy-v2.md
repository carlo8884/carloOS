## "Stealth mode" interpretation

Carlo's framing: "not really about launching websites anymore. It's really preparing those websites in stealth mode and making them potentially the highest and best use traffic sites and free sites that can generate revenue."

Operationally this means:
- **No PR pushes, no Product Hunt launches, no "we're live" announcements** — buyers value sites that look organically-grown, not VC-pumped.
- **Don't loud-publish handoff docs to public channels.** Keep ops/handoffs/ as internal coordination.
- **Make the sites look 5 years old** when they sell — established editorial team, deep content, real traffic compounding. The buyer is paying for *steady-state*, not *launch-mode hockey stick*.
- **Free to user, monetized via affiliate / insurance / ads** — no paywall, no friction. Sale value comes from traffic + revenue, not user count.

This is **actually the right play for a $10-25M exit on each site.** Hockey-stick launches sell to VCs at higher multiples but require ongoing growth pressure. Steady-state editorial+affiliate sites sell to strategic buyers (NYT, Dotdash, pet vertical rollups) at solid multiples without requiring further investment from Carlo.
