# Visual brief — premium homepage redesign pass

Carlo's directive (2026-06-01): the priority sites are credible but still feel like generic content
sites. Before launch each must feel like a **premium category destination** — the owner of its
one-word domain, not an article library. **Visual leads this.**

## Your deliverable, in order
1. **ONE premium first-screen standard FIRST** — a reference design + spec, not 8 one-offs. It must
   define: the hero system (image/video, identity-in-2-seconds), the homepage-as-product pattern (a
   real interactive tool/workflow on the first or second screen — not a link to one), the
   "what-you-can-do-here" hub framing, and per-domain differentiation of type/color/motion/photo
   treatment so no two sites feel templated. **Submit it to CSRO for sign-off against the launch bar
   before applying.**
2. **Apply the standard to the priority 8.** Per-site first-screen direction (hero + signature
   homepage product) is specified in `ops/csro/premium-domain-launch-bar.md` — build to that table.
3. **Fold in the open imagery P0s** as part of this pass: Ferret real-ferret hero (currently a dog),
   Saddle differentiated hero (currently identical to Horses), Lizard logo, horses-disciplines query.

(3) a product on the homepage not a link, (4) product-style architecture, (5) distinct visual
identity, (6) one memorable signature element, (7) survives a Tier-1 buyer demo.
