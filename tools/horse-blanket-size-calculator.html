How to measure (30 seconds, one tape, one helper)
  1. Stand the horse square on level ground.
  2. Start the tape at the center of the chest (the muscle between the front legs).
  3. Run it across the point of the shoulder, along the barrel, keeping it level.
  4. Finish at the point of the buttock — the very back edge of the hindquarters, by the tail.
  5. Read the number in inches. That's your size. Measure twice.

This is an estimate by build, not an exact fit. Always measure chest-to-tail before buying — it's the single biggest reason blankets get returned.

Sizes are US (inches) with UK equivalents shown. US and UK blanket sizing measure the same way — the UK label is just the same length in feet & inches (e.g. 72" = 6'0"). Blankets come in 3" increments; when a measurement lands between sizes we round up so the blanket fully covers the hindquarters.
