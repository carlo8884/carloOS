Fill-weight terms: Sheet = 0g (rain/wind only) · Lightweight ≤150g · Medium 200–250g · Heavy 300g+. Turnout blankets must be waterproof; stable blankets are not.

Recommendations use overnight low temperature, clip status, and condition. "Turnout" = waterproof outdoor blanket; "stable" = indoor only. When in doubt between two weights, a layerable lightweight + medium liner beats one heavy blanket for flexibility.
