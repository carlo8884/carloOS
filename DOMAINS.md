## Maintenance protocol

- **Add a domain:** append under the correct sub-cluster. Update sub-cluster count, group count (Pets/Horses), and in-scope total.
- **Remove a domain (sold or dropped):** move to a "Sold / Released" section at the bottom of this file with date and disposition. Do not delete — exit history matters for thesis review.
- **Re-bucket a domain:** edit in place; note the change in a one-line entry at the bottom of this file under "History."
- **Disagreement about bucketing:** IR Bot flags as a finding; CSRO decides; Carlo can override.
