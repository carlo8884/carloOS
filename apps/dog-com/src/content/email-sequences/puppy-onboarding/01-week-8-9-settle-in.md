If your puppy is 8 or 9 weeks old, the goal this week is not training. It's **structure** — and the foundation of structure is sleep.

**Sleep first.** Puppies 8–10 weeks need 16–18 hours of sleep per day in 1–2 hour naps, separated by ~1 hour of awake time. Anything less and you get an overtired puppy, which looks identical to a "hyper" or "bitey" puppy. Use the crate for every nap.

**Name recognition.** Say the name once. The moment the puppy looks at you, mark ("yes!") and reward with a single piece of kibble. Twenty reps a day, scattered through the day. That's it. You're not teaching commands yet — you're teaching that hearing the name = good thing happens.

**First vet visit.** This is the one appointment that matters more than any other. Goal: the puppy enters the clinic, gets handled, gets a treat, and leaves with a positive association. Schedule a meet-and-greet (no exam, no shots) BEFORE the first vaccination visit if your clinic allows. The vaccination visit then becomes "the place I get treats and meet nice people" instead of "the place I get jabbed".

**What you can skip this week.** Sit. Down. Tricks. Walks on a leash outside (parvo risk before DAPP-2). They all come — but later.
