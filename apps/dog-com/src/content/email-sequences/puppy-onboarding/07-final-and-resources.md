**One last thing.** You're going to come across confident-sounding training advice online that contradicts everything in this course. "Show them who's boss." "Alpha roll." "Make them earn everything." The dominance/pack-leader framework was based on flawed 1970s research on captive wolves and was retracted by the original researcher decades ago. AVSAB, AAHA, and modern veterinary behaviorists are unanimous: positive reinforcement is more effective, lasts longer, and produces a more stable dog. When in doubt, the source is the standard — not the volume.
