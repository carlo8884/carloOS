A reader named Priya sent us her week-by-week diary after her Lab mix Otis turned one. With her permission, we turned it into a piece on the site. The condensed version:

**Week 8.** Otis came home on a Saturday. Priya took the first week off work. The whole week was sleep, name games, and one tiny trip to the driveway to watch cars from her lap. No visitors. No park. Just "this house is safe."

**Week 9.** Two friends came over, one at a time, sat on the floor, didn't reach for him. Otis approached. Otis retreated. Otis approached again. Priya didn't intervene.

**Week 10.** First puppy class. Otis hid behind her chair for the first 20 minutes. By minute 30 he was on his back having his belly rubbed by the instructor. Priya almost cried.

**Weeks 11–13.** The "ten new things" rule. Umbrellas opening. The vacuum on. Walking on a metal storm grate. A man in a hi-vis vest. A toddler. A skateboard rolling past. Each one short, each one optional.

**Week 14.** First vet visit that wasn't shots. Just a "happy visit" — treats at the desk, then home. The vet tech loved her.

**Week 15.** Crate-trained, sleeping through the night, recall working in the yard.

**Week 16.** Adolescence started rolling in three weeks later — and most of it bounced off, because the foundation was already in.

What Priya kept saying in the diary: "I did less than I thought I should. And it worked."
