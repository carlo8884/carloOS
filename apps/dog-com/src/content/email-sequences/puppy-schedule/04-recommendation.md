Most "puppy starter kit" lists run 30 items. Most of those 30 things gather dust by week 12. After years of editing this category, here's the kit that actually earns its place — seven items, in priority order.

**1. A wire crate with a divider panel.** The divider lets you shrink the crate while the puppy is small, so they don't pee in the back corner. Single most important item in the house.

**2. An ex-pen.** A puppy playpen extension off the crate gives you a safe "out of crate but not loose" zone — the secret to actually getting work done.

**3. A 6-ft flat lead** (covered Wednesday — same recommendation).

**4. A WSAVA-compliant puppy food.** Royal Canin, Purina Pro Plan, Hill's Science Diet, Iams, or Eukanuba — these are the brands that meet the WSAVA Global Nutrition Committee's published criteria for veterinary nutritionist involvement and feeding trials. Large-breed puppy formula if expected adult weight >50 lb.

**5. Two real food puzzles** — not chew toys. A Kong Classic stuffed and frozen, and a snuffle mat. These do the mental work that a tired puppy needs to nap.

**6. Enzymatic cleaner** (not soap, not vinegar — actual enzyme). For accidents. You will need it.

**7. Pet insurance, signed up before the first vet visit.** Most policies exclude anything diagnosed before enrollment. We've covered the comparison in detail; the short version is Lemonade and Pumpkin both score well for new puppy owners.
