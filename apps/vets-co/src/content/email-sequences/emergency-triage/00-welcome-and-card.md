## Why this matters

Most pet emergencies are not actual emergencies.

Pet ER visits in the US run $400–$1,200 before any treatment. Owners frequently rush to the ER for something that could have waited until morning — and then can't afford the ER visit two months later when something genuinely catastrophic happens.

The point of the card is to give you a fast, calm framework. Most of the time, the answer is "this can wait until morning." Sometimes, it's "leave now." Knowing the difference is the single biggest financial — and emotional — protection you can give yourself as a pet owner.

## The one-line rule

If you can name a specific ER-NOW symptom your pet has, go. If you can't, you almost certainly have time to call first.
