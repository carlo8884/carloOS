Two weeks since you downloaded the checklist. We hope it's already lived in your tack trunk.

Two asks:

**1. Send us a photo.** Hit reply, attach a side-on photograph of your saddle on your horse — girthed loosely, no pad, no rider, horse standing square on level ground. If you can include a second photo from directly behind the cantle looking forward, even better. Tell us your horse's breed and your discipline. We read every reply and we'll come back with a one-paragraph read on whether anything in the static fit looks worth checking with a professional.

We do not diagnose at distance. We will tell you "looks reasonable, no obvious flags" or "the points look forward of the scapula — worth getting a fitter to confirm." That's the most a photograph can support.

**2. Tell us what the checklist didn't answer.** Western fit? Children's ponies? Treeless? Endurance? The questions that arrive most often become the next deep guide.

That's the email. Reply.
