Welcome. Your **12-Point Saddle Fit Checklist** is ready:

[Download the PDF →](https://saddle.com/downloads/12-point-saddle-fit-checklist.pdf)

Print it on cardstock if you can. It belongs in your tack trunk.

The twelve points cover, in order: wither clearance, gullet width at the points, tree angle versus shoulder angle, panel contact (the four corners), channel width over the spine, billet alignment, balance point, length behind the 18th rib, panel symmetry, flocking condition, the front-to-back rock test, and the static-versus-dynamic check (girthed and ridden).

Each point on the checklist is binary: it either passes or it doesn't. A saddle that fails any single point doesn't fit — full stop. The trade-off is which failure you can live with for which length of time, and that decision belongs to a professional fitter, not to a checklist.

A note up front: this checklist is for **diagnostic triage**. It tells you whether a saddle is in the conversation. It does not replace a qualified fitter's hands-on assessment. A saddle that looks correct on twelve points can still rotate, pinch, or bridge in motion — that's what the dynamic check is for.
