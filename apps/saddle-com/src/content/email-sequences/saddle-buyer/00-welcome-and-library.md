You signed up on Saddle.com. Welcome.

This is a buyer-side reference site — not a marketplace, not a sales funnel, not a brand-owned content channel. The job is to help you make better saddle, tack, and horse-keeping decisions with sourced information and editorial picks that disclose how they are chosen.

Over the next three weeks you will get four more short emails. Each one is a single decision real buyers have to make — fit, used inspection, brand-fit personality, what the discipline question actually changes — drawn from public standards (Society of Master Saddlers, Master Saddlers Association) and from peer-reviewed saddle-pressure-mapping research.

---

**What lives on Saddle.com today**

- **Fit guides.** The 4-point fit check, seat-size sizing, bridle and bit fit, used-saddle inspection. Owner-level checks plus when each tips into "call a fitter."
- **Reviews.** Discipline-tagged saddle reviews by horse build, plus the supporting equipment (helmets, blankets, stirrups, gloves). Documented-spec comparisons; the methodology is disclosed on every review.
- **Care guides.** Leather care, grooming, daily and seasonal routines. The fundamentals that extend gear life by years.
- **Horse-care reference.** Nutrition, body condition scoring, first-aid, trailer loading — the working horse-owner library.

---

**What this email sequence will not do**

- Push you toward a specific brand.
- Promise a perfect saddle exists.
- Replace a real saddle fitter.

What it will do: tell you how to be a better participant in the buying process, what the public research actually says, and which Saddle.com reference pages to bookmark.

— Saddle.com Editorial
