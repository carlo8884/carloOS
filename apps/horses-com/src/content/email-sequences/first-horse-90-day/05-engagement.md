Two weeks in. You're somewhere between the "I cannot believe this is mine" and "what have I done" phase. Both phases are normal. Both pass.

Two asks:

**1. Tell us about your horse.** Hit reply with: their name, age, breed (or guess), how you came to own them, and one thing you've already noticed about their personality. We read every reply. We do not respond to every one (we'd never sleep), but the stories shape what we write next.

**2. Tell us what the roadmap left out.** First-show prep? Trailer-loading a hesitant horse? How to interpret a colic episode? When to call the vet versus when to wait? Reply with the question. The most-asked become the next deep guides.

That's the email. Reply.
