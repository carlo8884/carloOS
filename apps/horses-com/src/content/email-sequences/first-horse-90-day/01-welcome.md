Welcome — and congratulations. Whether the horse is already in your yard or you're 90 days out from collecting them, your **First-Horse 90-Day Roadmap** is ready:

[Download the PDF →](https://horses.com/downloads/first-horse-90-day-roadmap.pdf)

The roadmap breaks the first three months into thirteen weeks. Each week has three things: one thing to **do**, one thing to **observe**, and one thing to **defer**. That third column is the one we wish someone had given us — most new horse owners burn money and energy in week 2 on tack they don't need yet, and skip the boring foundations they actually do.

Quick reassurance up front: this is normal. Every horseman you've ever met got something wrong in their first 90 days. The roadmap is designed to keep the things that go wrong from being the ones that cost real money or break trust with the horse.

A short orientation:

- Weeks 1–4: settle, observe, build the routine
- Weeks 5–8: vet, farrier, dentist baseline; introduce work gradually
- Weeks 9–13: assess what you actually need, plan the year ahead

Over the next two weeks, four more emails:

- Day 5: a real owner's story of buying her first horse at 38
- Day 9: the starter tack list that actually earns its place
- Day 14: tell us about your horse — we'd love to hear

You're going to be fine.
