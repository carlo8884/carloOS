You signed up for the Horses.com First-Horse Owner&apos;s 90-Day Roadmap. Welcome.

If you&apos;re about to buy your first horse, or just brought one home in the last week, this 8-email series walks you through the first 90 days week by week. Eight emails. Twelve weeks. Each email is paced to the decisions you&apos;re most likely to be working through that week.

**The roadmap, at a glance:**

- **Pre-purchase.** Pre-purchase vet exam (PPE), budget, barn shortlist.
- **Weeks 1–2.** Settling in. Gradual feed transition. Baseline vitals. Low-stress turnout.
- **Weeks 3–4.** Routine. Farrier on a 6-week cycle. First wellness vet visit. Daily ground manners.
- **Weeks 5–8.** Partnership. Ridden or driven work. Saddle and bridle fit. Trainer relationship.
- **Weeks 9–12.** Confident ownership. First-aid kit complete. Emergency plan written. Vitals known cold.

**What you can do today (no matter what week you&apos;re in):**

1. Write your horse&apos;s daily ration on an index card and pin it inside the feed-room door. You should be able to recite this from memory by the end of week 2.
2. Start a baseline. Heart rate at rest, respiration at rest, temperature, manure character, water intake. Five minutes. Note it.
3. Find the closest 24-hour equine referral hospital and save the number in your phone now. Not when you need it.

That&apos;s it for today. Next email arrives in three days and covers the single highest-leverage decision in the entire 90 days: the pre-purchase exam.
