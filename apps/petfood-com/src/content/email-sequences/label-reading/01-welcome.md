Welcome. Your free **Reading the Pet Food Label** guide is ready:

[Download the PDF →](https://petfood.com/downloads/reading-the-pet-food-label.pdf)

The guide walks through every section of a US pet food label in the order it appears on the bag: the brand line, the product name (where "with chicken" means something legally different from "chicken"), the guaranteed analysis, the ingredient list, the AAFCO statement, the feeding directions, and the manufacturer information.

Two principles up front. The first: **a label tells you what a manufacturer is willing to declare under FDA / AAFCO labelling rules.** It does not tell you whether the food is good. The second: **the single most informative line on the label is the AAFCO nutritional adequacy statement** — specifically whether it was determined by "formulation" or "feeding trial." That one distinction is the closest thing the industry has to a quality signal in plain English.
