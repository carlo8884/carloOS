You signed up for the PetFood.com Label Decoder. Welcome.

**The one-page decoder is attached** (`petfood-label-decoder.pdf`). Print it. Take it to the pet store. It covers the six panels that any commercial bag in the United States is legally required to carry — product name, guaranteed analysis, ingredient list, AAFCO statement, caloric content, feeding directions — and what each one is actually saying once you strip the marketing copy off.

Most pet food marketing is misleading. Not necessarily false — most of it is technically allowed under AAFCO model regulations and FDA labeling rules — but built to obscure rather than clarify. "Premium," "holistic," "human-grade," "super-premium," "biologically appropriate," "ancestral" are unregulated terms. The label tells the real story, but only if you know which boxes carry enforceable meaning and which boxes are marketing space.

I won't recommend a brand. The point of the sequence is that by the end of it, you can rank brands yourself.
