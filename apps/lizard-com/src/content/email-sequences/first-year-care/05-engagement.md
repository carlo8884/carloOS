Two weeks in. By now your enclosure should be set up — or you've decided not to get the animal until it is, which is the right call.

Two asks:

**1. Send us an enclosure photo.** Hit reply with: a clear photograph of the full enclosure (lights on, front panel open or removed), a second photograph of the basking spot from the animal's eye level, and the species you keep (or are about to). We'll write back with the two or three things we'd change. We do not shame setups. Every enclosure on the site started worse than yours.

**2. Tell us what the schedule didn't cover.** Brumation? Egg-laying females? The transition from juvenile to subadult feeding ratios? Specific calcium / D3 dosing? Vet-finding when you're far from a reptile vet? Reply with the question. The most-asked become the next deep guide.

That's the email. Reply.
