A reader named Sam shared a 12-month photo log of his first bearded dragon, Mango. With his permission, the condensed timeline:

**Month 1.** Mango arrived at ~6 weeks old, 4 inches snout-to-vent, in a 40-gallon breeder enclosure that was already too small for the year he had ahead. Sam knew this on day one. He had a 4'×2'×2' on order; it arrived in week 3. He ate roaches three times a day.

**Month 2.** First shed. Patchy, slightly retained on a back toe. Soak in shallow lukewarm water for 10 minutes solved it. Sam did not pull the shed.

**Month 3.** First Solarmeter reading: 2.4 UVI at basking, with a brand-name 10.0 bulb. Below the target band. The fix was lowering the fixture 4 inches; the next reading was 3.6.

**Month 5.** Mango stopped eating for nine days. Sam panicked. The vet checkpoint at month 5 caught it: temperatures had been creeping low because a thermostat probe had migrated. Probe re-secured, basking spot back to 105 °F, appetite back within four days. This is the scary month every new keeper has.

**Month 7.** Mango doubled in length. First "beardie tantrum" — a black-bearded display at the camera. Sam laughed and stopped photographing for a week.

**Month 9.** Mango refused greens for three weeks. Then started eating them again. Bearded dragons have opinions.

**Month 12.** Mango weighed in at 405 g, 17 inches end-to-end, alert, basking, glossy. The 12-month UVB bulb came out on the dot.

What Sam said at the end of the log: "Month 5 is the one. Get to month 6 and you've earned the dragon."
