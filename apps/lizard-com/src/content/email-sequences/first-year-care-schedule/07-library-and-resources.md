**One last thing.** You will come across confident-sounding reptile-husbandry advice — on forums, on pet-store packaging, on social media — that contradicts the schedule. "You don't need UVB for leopard geckos." "Crickets are fine without dusting if you gutload." "Snakes don't need a thermostat, just a UTH." Sometimes these are oversimplifications of a real point; sometimes they are wrong; sometimes they are a marketing pitch attached to the wrong product.

The standard for this site, and for the schedule, is the published literature: Mader's *Reptile Medicine and Surgery* (3rd ed.); ARAV (Association of Reptile and Amphibian Veterinarians); the *Journal of Herpetological Medicine and Surgery*; Solarmeter's published UV-index measurements. When in doubt, the source is the standard — not the volume.
