Over the next eight weeks I'll send one short email per phase — each timed roughly to where a typical fishless cycle is at that point, with the one thing that matters most that week.

**Reset your expectations now.** A full aquarium cycle takes **4 to 6 weeks** end-to-end. Sometimes longer. The pet-store advice of "wait a week and add fish" is the single biggest reason new tanks crash — it adds livestock before the nitrifying bacteria have colonised the filter media in numbers large enough to handle the ammonia load. Slow is the whole strategy.

**This week (whatever day you're on):** if you haven't already, set up the tank, the filter, the heater, and the substrate. Run the filter for 24 hours dechlorinated and dosed with a source of ammonia (more on sources in email #2). Then start daily testing. Don't add fish.

— Fish.com Editorial
