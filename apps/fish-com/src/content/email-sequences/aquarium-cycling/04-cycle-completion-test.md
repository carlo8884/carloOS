You're somewhere around week three. Ammonia is processing fast. Nitrite has likely peaked and started falling. Nitrate is climbing visibly. You're close — but "close" is not "cycled".

**The three reads that say the tank is ready.**

Test 24 hours after your most recent ammonia dose. The cycle is complete when **all three** are true:

1. **Ammonia = 0 ppm.**
2. **Nitrite = 0 ppm.**
3. **Nitrate is measurable** (typically 5–40 ppm depending on dosing history).

If ammonia is at 0 but nitrite is still showing anything above 0, you are not cycled. The most common mistake at this stage is reading "ammonia: 0" and concluding "done". The full pipeline only exists when nitrite also reads 0.

**The 24-hour re-dose test (the gold standard).**

This is the test that confirms the bacterial colony is large enough to handle a full fish load — not just the ambient trickle.

1. Dose the tank to 2 ppm ammonia.
2. Wait 24 hours.
3. Test ammonia and nitrite.

If both read 0 after 24 hours and nitrate has climbed, the tank can process a 2-ppm ammonia load in a day. That's enough for a sensible starting stock. **Repeat the test on two consecutive days** before stocking — one passing test could be a fluke; two in a row is a colony.

**A small note on nitrate.** Once cycled, your nitrate will keep climbing forever in the absence of water changes (live plants consume some, but most tanks don't have enough plant mass to zero it out). That's why nitrate management is the lifelong job — covered in email #6. For now, do a **50% water change immediately before stocking** to bring nitrate down to a reasonable starting level (ideally under 20 ppm), then add fish the same day or the next morning so the colony doesn't starve.

**Before you stock, the pre-flight checklist.**

- Heater set to species-appropriate temperature, holding steady.
- Filter running for at least three weeks on this water.
- 24-hour re-dose test passed twice.
- Large water change done to lower nitrate.
- Dechlorinator on hand for the new water you'll add.
- Drip-acclimation kit or a clean jug + airline + clamp ready.

**This week:** run the 24-hour re-dose test. If it passes twice in a row, you're ready for email #5 — first stocking, which lands next week. If it doesn't pass yet, keep testing daily and don't rush. A few extra days now buys you a year of stable tank.
