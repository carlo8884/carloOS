Two weeks in. If you started a cycle the day you signed up, you're somewhere around day 14 — the nitrite-spike phase. If you're cycling a planted tank or a reef, you might be earlier.

Two asks:

**1. Send us your parameters.** Hit reply with: tank size, days since first ammonia dose, current ammonia / nitrite / nitrate / pH / KH (and SG + temperature if saltwater). We'll send back a one-line read — "on track / stalling, here's why / done, you can add fish." We do this for every reply.

**2. Tell us what the kit didn't cover.** Quarantine procedure? Stocking math? How to interpret a phosphate reading? The most-asked questions become the next deep guides.

That's the whole email. Reply.
