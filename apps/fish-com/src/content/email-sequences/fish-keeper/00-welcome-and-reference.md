You signed up on Fish.com. Welcome.

Fish.com is a working aquarist reference — the cycling process, the equipment choices, the species pages, the water-quality framework. Both freshwater and saltwater. Editorial team voice, drawing on established aquarist practice and peer-reviewed work where it exists. Not a marketplace; not a livestock supplier.

Over the next three weeks you will get four more short emails — each on a single decision real aquarists have to make: cycling, equipment, stocking, and water testing.

---

**What lives on Fish.com today**

- **Cycling.** The nitrogen cycle — why it decides whether your fish live or die, the fishless vs fish-in cycling protocols, how to read the test kit during cycling.
- **Species pages.** Practical species references — tank size, water parameters, temperament, compatibility, common health issues. Both freshwater and marine.
- **Equipment.** Filters, heaters, lighting, CO₂ for planted, protein skimmers for marine. Selection by tank type and bioload.
- **Water chemistry.** Parameters by water type, testing cadence, how to interpret an out-of-range reading.
- **Saltwater / reef.** Aquascaping, livestock selection, the specific equipment and water-quality demands of marine systems.

---

**What this email sequence will not do**

- Tell you which fish to stock — that depends on your tank size, your filter, your water chemistry, and your goals.
- Endorse a specific equipment brand without context — selection depends on tank size and use case.
- Replace research before any specific purchase decision.

What it will do: anchor the four most common new-aquarist decision areas to public sources and to the Fish.com reference pages.

— Fish.com Editorial
